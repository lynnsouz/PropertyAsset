The Service currently provides the following Roles. Each Role includes the permissions of the Roles below it.

| Role | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | View the properties, transactions, reports and documents in the Workspace. Cannot create, change or delete records. |
| **Editor** | Everything a Viewer can do, plus: add and edit properties and transactions, upload and delete documents, delete transactions, and restore transactions and documents from Recently Deleted. |
