Built for people who own property together

Spreadsheets break the moment there's a second owner, a second currency or a second unit. Property Asset was built around all three.

Every property in one place

Houses, apartments, commercial space and land, with units, addresses, purchase price and current value.

Shared ownership, done right

Co-owners with shares that add up to 100%, per-transaction splits, and a My share view of what's actually yours.

Any currency

Recurring items

Dashboard and reports

Portfolio value, income, expenses and net for any period, per property or in total, with CSV export.

Documents

Questions

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon. Your data syncs between them.

Can my partner or accountant see the numbers?

Yes. Invite them to your workspace as an editor or a viewer. Viewers can see everything but change nothing.

What happens if I'm offline?

You can keep viewing and editing. Changes are saved on the device and sent when you're back online.

Which currencies are supported?

All major currencies. Rates come from the European Central Bank's reference rates and you can override any of them.

Where is my data stored?

With our hosting provider in the European Union. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open the Workspace tab and tap Delete Account. You can export your data first with Export Data. See Support for more.
