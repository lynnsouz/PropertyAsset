Common questions

I didn't get the verification code

Check your spam or junk folder. Codes expire after a while: tap Resend code on the verification screen to get a new one.

I forgot my password

On the Sign In screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email address. We'll email you a 6-digit code: enter it with your new password and you're signed in.

How do I invite someone to my workspace?

Open the Workspace tab and tap Invite Member to send an email invitation, or create an invite link or code. Owners and admins can change roles, and revoke or resend invitations under Invitations & Codes.

Why is an amount waiting for confirmation?

Recurring items set to confirm first wait until you check the amount. Confirm them on the Transactions tab. Waiting items aren't included in reports until they're confirmed.

I edited something offline and see a conflict

Someone else changed the same item before your change was sent. Open Workspace → Sync and choose which version to keep.

I deleted something by mistake

Deleted properties, transactions and documents stay in Workspace → Recently Deleted for 30 days. Owners and admins can restore them from there.

How do I export my data?

The Dashboard's share button exports the report as CSV. Workspace → Export Data creates a ZIP file with spreadsheets of properties, transactions, recurring items and members, plus every uploaded document.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Workspace → Delete Account. If you own a workspace other people use, transfer ownership first: open the workspace, go to Members and choose Transfer Ownership. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
